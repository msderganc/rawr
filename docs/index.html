Scores are geometric means where WinRAR is 100 and higher is better. Overall weights speed 70%, resources 20% and size 10%; resources is peak memory and CPU time, weighted equally.

  • Twelve operations per data set: creating at Fast, levels 2, 4 and 5, and Normal; creating solid at Fast, level 5 and Normal; then extracting and testing a plain and a solid archive. Both sides read the same archives, made by WinRAR.
  • Best of 3 runs. Every archive rawr made was checked with UnRAR.
  • Intel Core i9-12900K, 64 GB, Windows 11, November 2026.
  • Measured with scripts/bench.ps1; the raw results are in bench.csv.
